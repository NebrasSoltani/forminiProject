🔧 Troubleshooting

Email not received?

  1. Check SPAM and Promotions folders in Gmail
  2. Whitelist no-reply@formini.tn in your email contacts
  3. Wait 5-10 minutes (emails can be delayed)
  4. Check var/log/dev.log for errors

💡 Payment Flow

When a user completes payment:

  1. Stripe webhook → /stripe/webhook (server-side)
  2. Payment marked as confirmed
  3. BrevoService sends email + SMS
  4. User redirected with success message
